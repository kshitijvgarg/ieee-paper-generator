Scholarships are a critical lever for educational access, yet most students
rely on word-of-mouth or ad-hoc searches across dozens of portals. The
resulting information asymmetry causes students to miss awards they qualify
for, while providers under-reach their target demographic.

This paper introduces a **recommendation system** designed to close that gap.
We describe three contributions: a curated database of scholarships, a hybrid
model combining collaborative and content-based signals, and an evaluation
against a popularity-only baseline.
